Questions

Before You Ask

Do I need equipment?

Two dumbbells (or one kettlebell) and a little floor space are enough for the whole program. A mat helps. Every exercise has a bodyweight alternative, so you can start with nothing and add equipment as you go.

How much time does it take each week?

Three sessions of about 45 minutes, plus an optional 10-minute mobility reset on rest days. That’s under three hours a week — less than most people spend scrolling in a day.

I’ve never lifted weights. Is this too advanced?

No — Module 1 is built for complete beginners. Every movement is filmed step by step, and the Coached and VIP tiers include form checks so Maya can correct you before bad habits form.

How do the coaching calls work?

Coached members join a weekly live video call (Thursdays, 7pm Ottawa time, recorded if you can’t make it). Bring questions, get form feedback, and train alongside your cohort. VIP members also get six private 1-on-1 calls, booked around your schedule.

What’s the refund policy?

14 days, full refund, no forms. Start the program, do the first two weeks, and if it’s not for you, email Maya within 14 days of enrolling. Monthly plans are cancelled, not refunded for months already used.

What happens after the 12 weeks?

You keep lifetime access to all modules, and Module 6 teaches you to build your own sessions. Most graduates move to the 2-session-a-week maintenance plan — or join the next Strong in 12 cohort at the alumni rate.
